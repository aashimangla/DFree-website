WEBSITE PERFORMANCE

Website Audit Checklist

A website audit helps identify problems that can affect usability, credibility, accessibility, discoverability and conversions.

01

Content & clarity

Visitors should quickly understand what the business offers, who it serves and what action they can take next.

  • Clear value proposition
  • Easy-to-understand service descriptions
  • Clear calls to action
  • Current contact information
  • No outdated or placeholder content
02

Navigation & usability

Visitors should be able to move through the website without having to search for basic information.

  • Logical navigation
  • Working internal links
  • Readable menus
  • Clear page hierarchy
  • Useful mobile navigation
03

Mobile experience

Test the website on different screen sizes, especially because navigation, forms and content can behave differently on mobile.

  • Responsive layout
  • Readable text
  • Buttons easy to tap
  • No horizontal scrolling
  • Forms work correctly
04

Performance

Review loading behaviour and identify large images, unnecessary scripts or other elements that may slow down the experience.

  • Optimised images
  • Efficient scripts
  • Reasonable page size
  • Fast interaction
  • Core Web Vitals review
05

Trust & credibility

A business website should give visitors enough information to understand who they are dealing with before they make contact.

  • About information
  • Real contact details
  • Client work where appropriate
  • Testimonials where genuine
  • Privacy and legal information
06

Conversion

Every important service page should give visitors a clear next step.

  • Visible CTA
  • Working contact form
  • Simple enquiry process
  • Relevant landing pages
  • Clear service pathways

SOCIAL MEDIA

Social Media Marketing Checklist

Effective social media starts with a clear business objective rather than simply posting more content.

01

Define the objective

Decide whether the campaign is intended to build awareness, generate leads, drive sales, support customers or strengthen the brand.

02

Know the audience

Identify who you want to reach, what problems they have and what information would be useful to them.

03

Choose platforms

Select platforms based on your audience, business type, content format and available resources rather than trying to be everywhere.

04

Create content pillars

Establish a small number of recurring themes such as education, product information, customer stories, behind-the-scenes content or industry insights.

05

Build consistency

Create a realistic publishing schedule that the business can maintain consistently.

06

Engage with people

Social media is not only a publishing channel. Monitor comments, messages and relevant conversations and respond appropriately.

07

Measure results

Track metrics connected to the objective, such as reach, engagement, website visits, enquiries or conversions.

08

Improve the strategy

Review performance regularly and adjust content, audience targeting and campaigns based on what the data shows.

Remember: A larger follower count does not automatically mean better business results. Measure social activity against the business objective you are trying to achieve.

CUSTOMER ACQUISITION

Lead Generation Guide

Lead generation is the process of attracting potential customers and giving them a clear way to express interest in your product or service.

01

Attract

Reach relevant people through search, social media, content, referrals, advertising and other appropriate channels.

02

Educate

Give visitors enough useful information to understand the problem you solve and why your offering may be relevant.

03

Convert

Provide a clear action such as an enquiry, consultation request, quote request, registration or purchase.

04

Follow up

Respond promptly and use an organised process for managing enquiries and moving qualified leads toward the next step.

Lead-generation essentials

Landing Page

A focused page designed around one specific offer or audience.

Lead Form

Collect only the information needed to understand and follow up on the enquiry.

Call to Action

Tell visitors exactly what they should do next.

Follow-up Process

Define who receives enquiries, how quickly they respond and what happens next.

Track the full journey: Do not measure only clicks or form submissions. Where possible, connect enquiries with qualified leads, opportunities and eventual business outcomes.

WEBSITE PLANNING

Website Cost Guide Canada

Website cost depends on the scope of the project, not simply the number of pages. Understanding the components makes it easier to compare proposals and plan a realistic budget.

01

Website type

A simple informational website has different requirements from an e-commerce store, membership platform or custom web application.

02

Design requirements

Custom visual design, branding, animations, interactions and responsive layouts can add design and development work.

03

Functionality

Features such as forms, booking, payments, accounts, dashboards, search, integrations and custom workflows affect scope.

04

Content

Copywriting, photography, video, product information and content migration can all contribute to project requirements.

05

Technology

A CMS-based site, custom application or e-commerce platform can have very different development and maintenance requirements.

06

Integrations

CRM, payment gateways, analytics, email, booking systems and other third-party integrations can increase complexity.

07

Maintenance

Budget separately for hosting, domain renewal, security, updates, backups, content changes and ongoing technical support where applicable.

08

Growth requirements

Consider whether the website needs to support additional products, users, locations, languages or functionality in the future.

Before comparing website quotes

01 Define the scope

Know what pages, features and integrations are actually required.

02 Separate one-time costs

Design and development are different from recurring hosting, maintenance or software subscriptions.

03 Ask what is included

Compare deliverables, revisions, content, integrations and post-launch support.

04 Plan for maintenance

A website is an ongoing digital asset, not necessarily a one-time expense.

Canada-specific note: There is no single standard website price for Canadian businesses. Scope and implementation approach determine the project cost. DIGITAL MARKETING

Small Business Digital Marketing Guide

Digital marketing works best when channels work together around a clear business objective rather than being treated as disconnected tasks.

01

Build

Establish a credible digital presence with a useful website, clear positioning, relevant content and accurate business information.

  • Website
  • Business information
  • Brand presence
  • Contact pathways
02

Reach

Help relevant audiences discover the business through appropriate organic and paid channels.

  • Search
  • Social media
  • Content
  • Paid advertising
03

Convert

Turn attention into meaningful business actions through clear offers, landing pages and straightforward conversion paths.

  • Landing pages
  • Lead forms
  • Calls / enquiries
  • Bookings or purchases
04

Measure

Review what people do after discovering your business and use that information to improve future activity.

  • Traffic
  • Leads
  • Conversions
  • Customer acquisition
05

Improve

Digital marketing should be an ongoing process. Keep testing messaging, channels, content and conversion paths.

  • Content optimisation
  • Landing-page improvements
  • Campaign adjustments
  • Retargeting where appropriate

Useful digital channels

Search

Help people discover your business when they are actively looking for relevant products or services.

Business Profile

Eligible local businesses can maintain their information, photos, reviews and customer-facing presence on Google Search and Maps. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Social Media

Build awareness, share useful content and communicate with your audience through relevant platforms.

Paid Advertising

Use paid campaigns when they make sense for the audience, objective and budget. Google provides advertising options across Search, Maps, YouTube and other properties. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Start with the business goal. Choose channels based on what you are trying to achieve, who you need to reach and what resources you can sustain. You do not need to use every digital channel.
